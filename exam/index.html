Examination rules — read before you start

  1. The test opens in full screen. Leaving full screen, switching tabs, switching apps or minimising the window counts as a violation.
  2. You are allowed 3 violations. On the last one your paper is submitted automatically with whatever you have written.
  3. Answers must be typed by you. Pasting or dragging text in from anywhere outside this page is blocked and every attempt is reported to your instructor.
  4. Cut, copy and paste still work inside the test, so you can move your own paragraphs around — use the ✂ ⧉ 📋 buttons or Ctrl+X / Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V.
  5. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, italic, lists and tables; the Ω button for symbols, √x for equations, and 🖼 to photograph and upload a diagram you drew on paper.
  6. Every keystroke is saved to the server as you write. If you refresh or lose connection briefly, reopen this page on the same browser to carry on with the remaining time. If that device will not come back, tell your instructor — they can let you resume on another one.
  7. The timer starts when you press Start Test and never pauses.
  8. Right-click, printing and developer shortcuts are disabled.
  9. Marks are not shown here. Your instructor releases them after evaluation.
  10. The Start button unlocks only after the reading time below has elapsed. That time is separate from your test duration.
