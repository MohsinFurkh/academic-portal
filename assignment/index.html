How this works
  1. Everything you type is saved automatically and you can continue from any device by signing in again.
  2. Answers must be typed here. Pasting or dropping text from outside this page is blocked and every attempt is recorded. Cut, copy and paste still work inside the assignment for your own text.
  3. Images, code, data and other files go in your own Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or GitHub. Share them as “anyone with the link can view” and add the link under Supplementary files. Nothing is uploaded here.
  4. In a group, one member edits a section at a time. Click a section to start editing it; your team-mates see your changes live and it is freed when you stop.
  5. Your instructor sees who wrote each paragraph, each member’s typing and editing history, and every member’s private contribution declaration.
  6. At the deadline the assignment closes automatically. Whatever is saved at that moment is what your instructor receives, even if nobody pressed Submit.

Supplementary files

Put images, code, datasets or anything else in your own cloud drive and add the link here. Set sharing to anyone with the link can view — your instructor cannot request access. Accepted: Google Drive/Docs, OneDrive/SharePoint, Dropbox, Box, GitHub/GitLab, Kaggle, Colab, Overleaf, YouTube and similar.

    Contribution

    Measured by this page, not typed by anyone. Visible to every member of the group and to your instructor. Words written counts the words in the current text under each member’s name — the member who last typed in a paragraph owns it.
